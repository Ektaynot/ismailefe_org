Thoughts

It was a fun drink. Very casual. It would go great with greasy food. I had a hard time describing this wine. It didn't have any extreme characteristics. Maybe that's why it was fun. It was relatively cheap compared to how good it tasted.

I think it would be a great wine for people who don't like wine. I had it with my dear friend Osman. I wouldn't recommend drinking this wine alone.
