* Bottle Info

- Wine: Frontera Merlot
- Producer: Viña Concha y Toro
- Vintage: NV
- Country / Region: Chile / Valle Central
- Grape(s): Merlot (Possibly a small amount of other grapes.)
- Style: Off-dry
- ABV: 12%
- Bottling Date: 16.09.2025
- Price: ₺810 ($16)
- Tasted on: 18.09.2026

* Appearance

- Color: Cherry Red
- Clarity: Clear
- Intensity: Medium

* Nose

- Intensity: Light-Medium
- Main aromas: Cherry
- Secondary notes: Plum and Orange

* Palate

- Sweetness: Off-dry
- Acidity: Medium
- Tannin: Low
- Body: Medium
- Alcohol: Low-Medium
- Flavor notes: Very fruity 
- Finish: Medium

* Overall

- Rating: 7/10
- Would buy again: Yes


* Thoughts

It was a fun drink. Very casual. It would go great with greasy food. I had a hard time describing this wine. It didn't have any extreme characteristics. Maybe that's why it was fun. It was relatively cheap compared to how good it tasted.

I think it would be a great wine for people who don't like wine. I had it with my dear friend Osman. I wouldn't recommend drinking this wine alone.
